Out of This World Hockey

A fast-paced, over-the-top arcade hockey game set on a festive Thanksgiving and Christmas snow/ice rink, featuring muscular super-fast skaters, tiny striped referees, between-the-legs dekes, trick shots, wrap-around scoring, a 10-goal / 45-second Mercy Rule, and The Golden Tinsel & Starlight Cup championship series.

Features

Holiday Snow & Ice Rink: Festive rink lighting, falling snow, goal creases, and holiday celebrations.

Milky Way Teams & Goofy Rosters: Choose from Milky Way-themed squads and shuffle goofy player and goalie names before dropping the puck.

Muscular Builds & Hyper-Speed Action: Heavy body checks, turbo boost, starlight overdrive, and high-velocity slap and wrist shots.

Trick Moves & Wrap-Arounds: Between-the-legs puck handling, highlight-reel trick shots (Between-the-Legs Snapper, Spin-O-Rama Ripper, and Michigan Scoop), and wrap-around scoring around the net.

Tiny On-Ice Referees: Scaled-down striped officials skating and dodging play alongside the muscular roster.

Configurable Periods & 10-Goal Mercy Rule: Switch period lengths between 3m, 5m, and 7m. If a team builds a 10-goal lead, the trailing team has 45.0 seconds to score or the game ends immediately by Mercy Rule.

Championship Trophy Tracking: Compete for The Golden Tinsel & Starlight Cup across series matchups.

Multi-Input Support: Play with keyboard, mouse, on-screen touch controls, or Gamepad API controllers.

Controls

Keyboard & Mouse

Move / Skate: WASD or Arrow Keys

Aim: Move mouse cursor across the rink

Slap Shot: Hold and release Space or Left-Click

Between-the-Legs Deke / Wrist Shot: Q

Trick Shot: R

Turbo Boost: Hold Shift (or Right-Click when Overdrive isn't ready)

Overdrive: E (when charged to 100%)

Pause / Resume: P or Esc

Fullscreen: F

Gamepad

Skate: Left Stick or D-Pad

Aim: Right Stick

Slap Shot: A or RT

Between-the-Legs / Wrist Shot: X or LB

Trick Shot: Y

Turbo / Check: B or RB

Overdrive: LT

Start / Pause: Start

Mobile & Tablet Touch

Use the on-screen virtual D-pad on the bottom-left and the TRICK, DRIVE, CHECK / BOOST, and SLAP SHOT buttons on the bottom-right.

Running & Hosting on GitLab

Because the game is built as a self-contained HTML5/JavaScript application (index.html), you can run it locally or publish it as a static site:

Local Playtest: Open index.html directly in a modern web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari), or serve the folder with any local static server.

GitLab Pages (Optional): Commit index.html and README.md to your default branch (such as main), and add a .gitlab-ci.yml pipeline to copy index.html into public/index.html for GitLab Pages hosting.
